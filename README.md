## 🧭 About Me

I'm a Business Analyst in the making — coming from a **Business Development & Sales background**, now translating that stakeholder-facing, deal-closing instinct into structured problem-solving: requirements gathering, process mapping, and data-informed decision-making.

I don't just ask *"what do you want built?"* — I ask *"what problem are we actually solving, and how will we know it worked?"*

- 🔭 Currently building a **BA portfolio**: BRDs, stakeholder analyses, and process documentation drawn from real business scenarios
- 📊 Sharpening **SQL, Excel, and Python** as analytical tools — not as an end goal, but as a means to better requirements and sharper insights
- 🎯 Working toward a **Master's in Business Analytics (NZ)** and ECBA → CCBA → CBAP certification path
- 🧩 Believer that a well-written BRD outlasts a flashy dashboard
